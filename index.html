About Us

Brick by brick,
carried from another era.

Divine Homestay sits above the Sahyadri valley, built from hand-laid stone and finished in carved teak. Every room keeps its own view of the hills, and the whole estate is offered to one group at a time.

From the veranda to the courtyard, the house is furnished the way an old family home would be — heavy wood, warm brass, carpets underfoot — not the way a hotel room usually is.

Dining
